`pair-original--calebisdrawing.jpg` is the untouched two-figure

crops of it, so the original composition is preserved alongside the split.

## Installing

Foundry can only show images that live inside its user data directory. Put
this folder there so the files sit at:

    Data/salt-run-ambush/art/<file>

The simplest route is Foundry's own file picker: open any actor's portrait,
click the folder icon, then Upload, creating the folders as you go. If you
put them somewhere else, change `ART_BASE` at the top of
`TBE-Salt-Run-Ambush-Installer.js` and nothing else needs touching.

A missing file costs you a portrait and nothing else. Anyone without art
keeps Foundry's default silhouette.

| File | Character | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `dunchadh-reave--` | Dunchadh Reave | Small, sour, fur hat, gold chain and rings, a once-fine cloak now patched. A man with standing he is protective of and finery that has seen better years. |

| `old-ambrose-duff--` | Old Ambrose Duff | One eye bandaged, heavy moustache, fur over armour, gold at the collar. The gold reads exactly like the one thing from the old life he refuses to sell. |
| `renn-kestrel--` | Renn Kestrel | Travel-worn practical kit, cloak, blade over the shoulder, a steady unshowy look. A quartermaster who reads ground rather than charges it. |
| `sela-voss--` | Sela Voss | Twin blades, a guarded expression, kit that is good but not showy. Strikes first, trusts slowly. |

## Not cast

Three pregens have no match in this set, and forcing one would be worse
than leaving the silhouette:

- **Dags Farrow.** Nothing here carries a bow, and he is defined by one.
- **Ysolt Vane.** No combat skills, a stolen fine cloak and a ledger. Every
  figure in this set reads martial.
- **"Mother" Mairwen Coll.** A healer-performer with a lute and a belt
  knife. The closest candidate is carrying a poleaxe.

## Unused, kept for later

- `unused-two-commanders--` two white-cloaked veterans. The bald swordsman
  is an alternative Ambrose if you want him grander and less battered.
- `unused-beaded-warleader--` scarred, beaded, axe on the back.
- `unused-elder-with-staff--` stout bearded elder with a carved staff.
  A good Riona, who never appears on-page but is what the Beat 5 Trial is
  actually fought over.
- `unused-raven-lord--` armoured, long-haired, with a raven.
- `unused-burning-arm--` dark lamellar, axe, one arm molten and glowing.
  Nothing in this module has a magic limb, but it is a strong hook.
- `unused-spectacled-scholar--` smoked glasses, long coat, cane.
