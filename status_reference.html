TBE Status & Peril Reference

Every status this pack can put on a token, grouped the way the book presents them (Ch.10 Combat, Ch.11 Wounds, Healing & Perils, Ch.14 Weave Magic). "Auto" means TBE: Attack, TBE: Wounds & Recovery, or TBE: Cast applies and clears it for you as part of the roll. "Manual" means nothing computes it yet — mark or clear it yourself from TBE: Status Effects when it comes up at the table. Open TBE: Status any time to see everything currently active on your character in one card, and to repaint the token icons if anything ever looks stale.
