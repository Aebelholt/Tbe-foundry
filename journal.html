The Broken Empires, solo in Foundry

Everything in this pack is a macro or a roll table. Nothing overrides your game system, so it works on top of Call of Cthulhu 7e, Custom System Builder, or anything else.

The loop

  1. Frame the scene. Run TBE: Subverted Scene, state your expectation, take what the die gives you.
  2. Act. Roll with TBE: Skill Roll or TBE: Opposed Roll.
  3. Ask when you don't know. Ask the Weave, with odds. Phrase the question so a Yes creates a complication.
  4. Interpret. Doubles on the oracle fire a Random Event automatically. Go with your first instinct.
  5. Keep the list current. Add NPCs, goals, and threads to TBE: Empires List as they matter, cross them off when they don't.

What the roll macros implement

  • Roll d100 under modified skill. Success Levels = the tens die, minimum 1 on any success.
  • Critical success: doubles under the skill, or the skill value exactly. Adds +3 SL.
  • Critical failure: doubles over the skill.
  • 01-05 always succeeds; 99-00 always fails. Skill 0 or less still succeeds on 01-04 and crits on 05.
  • Skill over 100 cannot critically fail and adds bonus SL equal to the tens digit of the excess, minimum 1.
  • Opposed rolls apply the full resolution ladder: SLs, then criticals, then higher die, then higher modified skill. Degree of Success = winner's SL minus loser's SL.
  • Fixed Numbers act as an automatic success at that many SL, and lose ties to a rolled skill.

Extended Rolls & Social Encounters

  • TBE: Extended Roll (Ch.2) — a task that takes more than one roll: crafting, research, tracking, anything with a target Success Level total and an interval. Start one, have each participant "make an attempt" during the interval, then "resolve the interval": the single highest successful roll counts in full, up to two more successful rolls that same interval each add +1 SL, and a critical failure anywhere in the interval halves the accumulated total (round up). Reaching the required SLs succeeds; running out an optional Limit of intervals fails.
  • TBE: Social Encounter (Ch.13) — the same engine applied to a scene. Static: a hidden Tolerance (how many attempts, success or fail, before the NPC ends the audience) and a running SL total compared to an outcome table once the scene ends. The same skill auto-fails on a third consecutive use. Exceeding Tolerance ends things immediately and knocks 1d10 off the total first. Competitive: two or more sides each track their own total; a successful roll adds to your own side or subtracts from a rival's. Two sides race to a Victory Condition and the winner's Degree of Success (winner minus loser) sets the outcome tier; three or more sides (a council vote, say) resolve by whoever has the most support when the GM calls it, no DoS table needed.
  • Both tools remember their state in the world between macro runs, so a negotiation or a months-long crafting project can span more than one session. The running totals are shown openly in the dialog and the chat card, the same as everything else in this pack — if you're running a group and want the book's Tolerance-is-secret rule to actually hold, keep this dialog to yourself the way you would a GM screen.

Solo house rules worth taking

  • Enhanced Defense, Solo and Solo Constitution (d20 Wound Die) free for your PC.
  • Lone Wanderer free if you use the full Travel rules.
  • +2 NPC Tolerance in static social encounters.
  • Hire NPC allies early. Solo combat is lethal.

Tables in this pack

TBE: Random Events, Event Randomizers I and II, Subverted Expectations, Opposing Difficulty, Empires List. Edit any of them freely, the macros read whatever is in the table.
