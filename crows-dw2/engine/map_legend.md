## Legend

1 character = 1 square (5 ft). `(x,y)`: x runs east, y runs south. Read x off the three ruler rows (sign, tens, ones). Diagonals count as 1 square (R18). Levels of one building share a grid, so the same (x,y) is directly above or below; a separate site (a crypt) has its own.

| Mark | Meaning | Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#` | wall or rock | `.` | floor |
| `,` | open ground | blank | the drop, or not yet seen |
| `+` | wooden door | `=` | metal door |
| `"` | shuttered window | `x` | iron fence (10 ft) |
| `/` | stairs or steps (same level) | `^` | raised landing |
| `<` `>` | stairs up / down to another level | `:` | open well: drop to the level below |
| `T` | table | `h` | shelf, cabinet, rack or wardrobe |
| `b` | bed or bunk | `c` | chest |
| `o` | barrel or sack | `F` | hearth |
| `r` | rug | `P` | pillar |
| `w` | weapon rack | `g` | open grave |
| `M` | skull mausoleum | `O` | hole |
| `*` | sigil on the floor | `S` | statue |
| `t` | dead tree | `k` | cart |
| `&` | boulder | `@` | the crow; other capitals are tokens |
