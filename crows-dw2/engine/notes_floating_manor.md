## Source quirks

- The printed map labels the dining hall "Area 3". The key says Area 2. Here, 2 is the dining hall and 3 is the kitchen.
- The kitchen spiral is drawn one square off from its top in area 7. Treat them as the same stair.
- The area 9 trapdoor isn't drawn on the source map. The key puts it under the curtained bed: `O` at upper (16,0).
- The outhouse shaft's landing in area 15 isn't marked. Pick a spot on first use and log it in the sealed ledger.
