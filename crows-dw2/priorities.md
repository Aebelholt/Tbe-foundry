# Priorities (review of the full parking lot and all source material), 2026-10-11

## 1. What the evidence says now
- **Rule discipline:** Pilots 01 and 02 gave real failures, all fixed on paper in v1.3. Pilots 03 to 05 gave almost no rules data because the player stopped.
- **Interest:** the one observed failure since is that the game went stale (same chapel twice, blank crow, nothing live in the fiction). Gold and visible progress raised interest. We never measured this before.
- **Untested since v1.3:** `Maneuver?`, one-reply rounds, `round`/`foe`, rule A (roll when at stake), Marks, scene/cover/flank, the card lookup.
- **Noise:** one trial of anything is within noise (Phase 3). Treat each pilot as a symptom, not a verdict.

## 3. Ranked
**P1: do now (small, cheap, aimed at the observed failure)**
1. **Live start** (text and sealed template): one Threat and one named person active at session zero, visible in the first scene. Fixes ethos and logos. No code.
2. **XP milestone line:** `ledger xp N`, status shows `TXP t, n to the next bonus`, `rest` announces a milestone and the three printed options (C6). Sourced, tool-owned, no Ref judgment. Fixes progress.
3. **Novelty:** the Ref opens each session at a site rolled from `table wyrd_locations` or the dungeon list, and may not reuse the previous session's site (a one-line `site` log). Uses data already built.
4. **Starter crows:** a few pre-made crows (backgrounds, gear placed, stats set) so session zero is one message and nothing is blank. Data and a short rule.
5. **Interest tick** in the checklist ("did something change?", "do I want the next reply?"). No build.

**P2: after one pilot that holds interest**
6. **Boons from ruins** (Stonetop arcana structure). Re-ranked up: it answers the "something to motivate the player" gap, gives rewards other than gold and a reason to return. Wait for step 1 to 3 to show whether they are enough.
7. **`beat up|down|ma` log** with one line of advice. Generalises two existing Nevers.
8. **Simplification: engine-rolled tier-1 move** (`engine move`). Removes a Ref decision.
9. **HTML card extractor** (Cowork) so spell and item numbers are real.

**P3: later or only on evidence**
10. Per-dungeon mark tables and escalating marks (Marks themselves are untested; three returns with Miasma take sessions).
11. Pre-keyed dungeons and an app-run loop (rungs 4 and 5; the long-term shape, large).
12. Card-deck artifact (player-side sheet).
13. Archivist pass at scene end (costs tokens).
14. Fold social moves into plain Mind tests; results in words.

**Park or drop**
- Sentence layer for non-combat (Realis): interesting, a different game; revisit with Nimble.
- Crit as a mark source, doom as a mark trigger, mundane/wyrd fallout categories, bestiary conversion, hex generator, Magic of the Wyrd: no evidence of need.
- Facing: rejected (not in Crows).

## 5. Method
- Add rules only for observed failures; start each idea in the parking lot.
- Score each pilot on two axes: rule discipline and interest.
- Two clean pilots before calling a text rule settled.
- Hot card ceiling about 900 words (now 859).
- Next pilot should be short (about 10 exchanges), with P1 items 1 to 4 in place, and a new site.
