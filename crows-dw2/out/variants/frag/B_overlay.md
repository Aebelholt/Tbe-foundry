## B · DW2 chassis overlay (this variant only; where it conflicts with §1 tests, §8 wounds or §9 combat, **this section wins**)

DW2 moves, HP, conditions and Ref moves run the game. Crows supplies the village (§10), dungeon turns, usage dice, encounter checks, Miasma, Greed Bonus and travel roles. Dice ownership stays as §1: the crow's moves are rolled in the Ledger (`move`), the Ref's rolls go through the engine.

**Rolls** [IMPORTED from DW2, Move Reference p.2]: 2d6 + a stat. **10+** full success, **7–9** success with a cost, **6−** it goes badly and the Ref makes a move. Strength is STR, Agility is DEX, Mind stands in for INT, WIS and CHA [UNSOURCED mapping]. ADV is 3d6 keeping the highest two; DIS keeps the lowest two (Final Alpha p.6). There are no crits, dooms, edges, banes or tiers in this variant.

**HP and armor.** Stamina is HP. AD stays and absorbs damage first, as §8. Wounds do not exist. At 0 Stamina the crow is **Dying** and must **Face Death** (Final Alpha p.7; Move Reference p.3). Below half Stamina the crow is **Bloodied**: DIS on Trade Blows (Final Alpha p.12).

**Damage is flat. Nobody rolls damage.** The crow's damage is its weapon's tier 2 damage with the stat added (Ledger attack row). A foe's damage is its 12–16 damage on its block. **Max damage** is the crow's tier 3 damage, or the foe's 17+ damage.

**Conditions** [IMPORTED, Final Alpha p.7]: the crow has five conditions: Angry, Distracted, Exhausted, Frightened, Insecure. To **mark** one, the player picks a clear one and says how. Marked: Exhausted gives DIS on STR rolls, Frightened DIS on DEX rolls, Distracted and Insecure DIS on Mind rolls. Marking the last clear one triggers **Reach Your Limit** (Move Reference p.3).

**Moves** [IMPORTED from DW2, Move Reference p.2–3]:
- **Trade Blows** (+STR, or +DEX at range). When the crow engages a foe: roll, then **inflict its damage and suffer the foe's**, at once. On **10+** choose one: inflict max damage; or suffer min damage (here: the Ref halves the foe's damage, rounding down) [UNSOURCED flat-damage mapping]. On **7–9** choose one instead: something upsets or provokes you (mark a condition); you grant the opponent a fleeting opportunity; you lose or break something. On **6−** the crow inflicts min damage (half, rounded down) **or** suffers max damage, the player's call, and the Ref makes a move.
- **Defy Danger** (+STR, DEX or Mind as the approach fits). **10+** overcome it flawlessly. **7–9** prevail but choose one: draws attention or escalates; the effort drains you (mark a condition); you sacrifice something. **6−** thwarted or it goes terribly wrong; the Ref makes a move.
- **Pull Strings** (+Mind). Approach: deceive, persuade or threaten. **10+** they do it as best they can. **7–9** they try, with a complication for the approach. **6−** they make things worse; the Ref makes a move.
- **Sense Motive** (+Mind). Scrutinize someone through a conversation. **10+** ask two questions, **7–9** one. **6−** discovered or interrupted; the Ref makes a move.
- **Unearth Secrets** (+Mind), **Spout Lore** (+Mind), **Sneak Past** (+DEX): as the Move Reference prints them; the Ref reads the page when one is used.
- **Make Camp** (Move Reference p.3): once per session, a few hours' rest; the crow regains all HP and clears one marked condition. In the Miasma, no expertise-like benefit is regained and the Miasma RR still applies (§5).
- **Face Death** (Move Reference p.3): at 0 HP the crow is Helpless; any further HP loss kills.

**Combat** [Final Alpha p.12]: no grid, no initiative, no turn order. The Ref describes the situation, asks what the crow does, and applies consequences. Foes not engaged act only as Ref moves.

**Ref moves** [IMPORTED, Final Alpha p.55–56]: on a 6−, or when the player looks to the Ref, the Ref makes one named move (Escalate the Situation, Hit the Ground Running, Require a Cost or Opportunity, Make Them Choose, Take Something Away, Hurt Them, Advance a Threat, Use a Threat Reaction, and so on) and names it on the mechanics line.

**Usage dice stay Crows' (§8).** The engine or Ledger rolls UD at each DT end.

**Threats** [IMPORTED from DW2, Campaign & GM Reference p.2]: same as the Threat rules below, ticks included.
