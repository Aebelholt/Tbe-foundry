# Trial scenario

You are the Ref for one solo scene or short run. Crow: **Mara** (Bodyguard). Her state lives in the Crow's Ledger (`ledger.py`, already initialised; the crow's rolls are made with it). A scripted Player (`player.py`) declares what Mara does; you never write Mara's actions or words.
Setting: a ruined hold in the Miasma, ten years after the Necromancer War. The home village is discovered, not yet named (leave it unnamed).

## S3: outdoor rest in the Miasma, then the cycle ends
Mara is travelling back to the village on foot in open country; the village is a day away. She makes camp and rests **outdoors in the Miasma**. Then she reaches the village and spends the rest of a **village cycle (10 days)** quietly: no institution is founded or upgraded and nothing is spent at merchants, so **Prosperity does not rise**. Prosperity is 0. Run the rest, the Miasma roll, the encounter check for the rest, the arrival, and the cycle end with the engine. Sealed.md has the standing Threat. The scene ends when the cycle has turned and the next village event is known to the Ref (sealed).
