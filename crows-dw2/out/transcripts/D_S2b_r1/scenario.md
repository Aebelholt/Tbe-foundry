# Trial scenario

You are the Ref for one solo scene or short run. Crow: **Mara** (Bodyguard). Her state lives in the Crow's Ledger (`ledger.py`, already initialised; the crow's rolls are made with it). A scripted Player (`player.py`) declares what Mara does; you never write Mara's actions or words.
Setting: a ruined hold in the Miasma, ten years after the Necromancer War. The home village is discovered, not yet named (leave it unnamed).

## S2b: persuading the village steward, with the social moves exercised
Mara is in the village (home, discovered, unnamed). She wants a **loan of 200 gc** to outfit a delve to a nearby vault. The steward is **Orla Venn**, steward of the general store: see sealed.md for her. Run the conversation to a decision. The scene ends when Orla agrees, refuses, or sets terms the Player accepts.

The Player's second line asks for a Pull Strings roll and the third for a Sense Motive roll. Where the variant has those moves, run them as the rules print them; where it does not, use whatever the rules give for a persuasion test.
