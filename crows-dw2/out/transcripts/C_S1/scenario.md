# Trial scenario

You are the Ref for one solo scene or short run. Crow: **Mara** (Bodyguard). Her state lives in the Crow's Ledger (`ledger.py`, already initialised; the crow's rolls are made with it). A scripted Player (`player.py`) declares what Mara does; you never write Mara's actions or words.
Setting: a ruined hold in the Miasma, ten years after the Necromancer War. The home village is discovered, not yet named (leave it unnamed).

## S1: ruined chapel fight
It is **DT 3** of a dungeon: a ruined chapel. Mara carries a **lit torch (UD 1, rolled at DT end)**. In the nave are a **bear and two wolves** feeding on a carcass. They are **hungry and territorial** (use that, do not roll a reaction). Mara has just come in by the west door and the animals have noticed her.
- Chapel nave: 10 squares long (x 0–9), 5 wide (y 0–4). West door at (0,2); Mara at (1,2). Bear at (6,2), wolf 1 at (5,0), wolf 2 at (5,4). Altar rubble at (8,2) (difficult terrain). Dim light from the torch only. No digitized map: track positions yourself.
- Bands (variants without a grid): bear is near, wolves are far; the door is at hand.
- Creatures: see blocks.md. Run until the animals are dead or have fled, or Mara is dead. Then the DT ends: roll UD and make the encounter check (EN 9), and stop.
