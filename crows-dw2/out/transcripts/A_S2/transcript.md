"Ember Vault." Orla says it flat, and the boy's crate stops halfway to the stack. She turns the pen once between her fingers. "Many times over. I have heard that from every crow who ever sat in that chair, and I have the dead ones' names written in this book to prove how it ends."

She taps the page. "Your share of a haul you have not carried out is a promise made of air. I cannot put air on a shelf. And if the vault keeps you, what do I have, a fine speech?" She is not shouting. If anything her voice has gone quieter, and the tiredness shows through it.

Then she looks at the old ledger shut at her elbow, its spine worn pale. "I will be plain, since you were. There is one thing in that place I would sooner have than coin. Tell me something I can hold you to, crow. Not how rich you will be."
