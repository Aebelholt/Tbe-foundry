# Creature blocks (verbatim numbers from the Ref Book)

## Bear (F16)
Size Large · Power 6 · Type Animal · Stamina 20 · Speed 6, climb 6 · Slots 10 · Agility 0 · Mind −2 · Strength 2
- Bite (+2) Melee 1: 12–16 4 dam · 17+ 6 dam
- Claws (+2) Melee 1 (2 targets): 12–16 2 dam · 17+ 3 dam
- Dangerous When Cornered: when the bear has 15 or lower Stamina, it deals an additional 2 dam with attacks.
- No Dim Light Penalty.

## Wolf (F21)
Size Medium · Power 3 · Type Animal · Stamina 10 · Speed 7 · Slots 5 · Agility 1 · Mind −2 · Strength 0
- Bite (+1) Melee 1: 12–16 3 dam · 17+ 4 dam
- Pack Hunter: the wolf gains a total +3 bonus to attacks (instead of +2) for flanking with another wolf.
- No Dim Light Penalty.

## Undead A (F34), "razorback"
Size Medium · Power 2 · Type Undead · Stamina 10 · Speed 5 · Agility 1 · Mind −3 · Strength 2
- Claws (+2) Melee 1: 12–16 2 dam · 17+ 4 dam*
- Spine (+2) Ranged 5: 12–16 1 dam · 17+ 3 dam
- *Lacerate: when the undead gets a tier 3 result with their claws against a creature and damages their Stamina or gives them a wound, the target gains a laceration until they regain Stamina. When the target takes a maneuver and an action on their turn, they take 1 P dam for each laceration they have.
- Leap: the undead can jump a distance equal to their speed as a maneuver.

## Undead B (F35), "longclaw"
Size Medium · Power 4 · Type Undead · Stamina 20 · Speed 6 · Agility 2 · Mind −2 · Strength 2
- Claws (+2)* Melee 1: 12–16 3 dam · 17+ 5 dam
- Spine (+2) Ranged 10: 12–16 2 dam · 17+ 4 dam
- *Lacerate: when the undead hits with their claws against a creature and damages their Stamina or gives them a wound, the target gains a laceration until they regain Stamina. When the target takes a maneuver and an action on their turn, they take 1 P dam for each laceration they have.
- Leap: as Undead A.
- Spiked Counter: when the undead counters, they deal 5 dam.
