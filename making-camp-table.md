# Making Camp (table copy, 0.6)

The One Ring 2E. Loremaster side. Core rests run unchanged (Core p.71). Camp adds one task per hero.

## Flow

1. **Player asks to camp.** The LM never calls it. Only when the Company is stopped in a played scene.
2. **Guide picks the camp.**
   - **Light camp:** Short Rest. The watch plus one other task.
   - **Full night:** Prolonged Rest. One task per hero, the watch included.
   - **Fire:** needed for Cook and Sing. Gives brands against *Fear of Fire* foes. In hostile lands it costs the watch (1d).
   - **Cold:** the watch gains (1d).
3. **Declare tasks** before anyone rolls. One sentence each: what they do and how.
4. **Cap:** at most **3** capped tasks, counting the watch. Capped: Keep Watch, Cook, Sing, Tell a Tale. Everything else is uncapped.
5. **Roll Pick the Spot first**, then the **watch**, then the rest.
6. **Rest as normal.** Zero Hope heroes regain 1 on a Prolonged Rest. Camp never counts as a safe refuge, so no Fatigue comes off here (Core p.111).
7. **Note boons and uses** with tokens.

**Dice cap:** camp effects give at most (1d) gained and (1d) lost on any roll. A gain and a loss cancel. Penalties are dice, never Ill-favoured.

## The watch (Look-out, Awareness, always rolled)

| Result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Success, 1+ tengwar | Clean. **Watchful Night.** |
| Success | Clean. |
| Failure | One soft complication. Rest still counts. |
| Failure + Eye | **Ambush** (Core p.95). Every hero rolls Awareness. Failures take no opening volley and no action in the first Close Quarters round. |

*Expert Ambusher* foes make the watch Ill-favoured. No watch at all: planned ambush with total surprise.

**Soft complications (pick one):**
- A curious watcher is near when the next scene opens.
- Wet wood: no fire tonight. (Skip if rain already hit the camp.)
- Something takes the food: **Good Meal has one use fewer**, or none if not yet cooked.
- Bad sleep on old ground: one hero makes a Shadow test (Valour) against Dread 1.
- Tracks round the camp at dawn.

## Capped tasks (boons end at next camp or Fellowship Phase)

| Task | Roll | Boon | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Keep Watch** | Awareness | **Watchful Night** (1+ tengwar): if foes start a fight in camp or the next scene, Might 1 attackers are Weary in round 1. 2+ tengwar: Might 2 too. Never Might 3. *Heartless* unaffected. | 1 |
| **Cook** | Hunting | **Good Meal:** one Protection roll Favoured. Needs fire and food. | 2 |
| **Sing** | Song | **Hearth Song:** after a Shadow test against Dread, reduce Shadow gained by 1. Needs fire. A song sung at the table gains (1d). A one-sentence description gains nothing. | 2 |
| **Tell a Tale** | Lore | **Old Tale:** ask one question about the place ahead. See below. | 1 |

## Uncapped tasks

| Task | Roll | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Pick the Spot** | Scout, Explore | **Snug Site:** the watch gains (1d), and if attacked, all heroes gain (1d) on Awareness against surprise. Failure: the watch loses (1d). Roll before the watch. Dice cap applies to the watch. |
| **Tend a Wound** | Healing, one Wounded hero | Counts as First Aid if they haven't had it (Core p.101). Their next Short Rest recovers Endurance as if not Wounded (house rule, Core says nothing). The Wound stays. |
| **Hold Each Other Up** | Enhearten or Insight | **Kindled Hearts:** Company regains 1 Fellowship point, not above rating. Once per Adventuring Phase. |
| **Stand Guard** | Awareness or Insight | No boon. Guards named people or one thing against night actions. Success: aimed-at harm is caught as it begins. Failure: it begins, the guard is first on the scene. |
| **Lend a Hand** | None | Give up your task. Another hero's camp task gains (1d). One helper per task. Dice cap applies. |

**Failure + Eye on any task:** one soft complication, unless the task says otherwise.
- Tend a Wound: hero loses (1d) on their next Strength roll.
- Hold Each Other Up: a quarrel, no Fellowship points spendable this camp.

## Old Tale (rolled openly)

- **Success:** true answer. The first roll using it is Favoured. Spent on that roll, whatever the result.
- **Failure:** true answer missing one material detail.
- **Failure + Eye:** one detail is false. The player knows something is wrong, not what. Deliver it confidently and play it straight.
- **Scope:** surface state only (terrain, routes, occupants, recent events). An Eye corrupts one detail and never widens.

| d6 | False detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Route:** the path ends at an obstacle or turns elsewhere |
| 2 | **Occupant:** the place is empty or held by someone else now |
| 3 | **Danger:** the threat moved area or season |
| 4 | **Landmark:** it fell or changed |
| 5 | **Condition:** the route is no longer passable this season |
| 6 | **Recent history:** something has troubled the place |

## Night actions (danger already inside the camp)

- **Watch success:** the scene opens as the act begins. The Company can step in.
- **Watch failure:** the scene opens mid-act. The Company wakes to it and can still act.
- Never resolve it off-screen. Stand Guard covers the guarded whatever the watch rolled.

## Hard limits

- Boons never remove a failure, stop a Wound or Piercing Blow, change a Feat die, give Hope directly, or remove Fatigue.
- No duplicate boons in one camp.
- Favoured is binary (two Feat dice, keep best). Favoured and Ill-favoured cancel to a single die. *Great/Horrible Strength* Ill-favoured Protection cancels Good Meal.
- An Eye on the Feat die still counts as zero, and still fails a Miserable hero.

## Campaign (*Fading Song of Arda*)

- Camp rolls are not journey rolls. No boon touches the Eye bonus Progress roll.
- The Restorer's Track clock doesn't pause for rest. Old Tale never answers about Narendil or the Track.
- Optional: if a hearth song tries to command (weather, ward, fear), let it work, then add 1 Shadow to the singer or a sign of attention next scene.
- If the Eye is ruled a Grace event, "failure + Eye" becomes plain failure everywhere.
- Lena's Stout-hearted: Hearth Song acts after the test, no overlap.
- Dunlending Survivor Virtues unchecked.

## Warts (known, unfixed, run it anyway)

- **Unverified against Core:** the Ambush effects in the watch table. Check p.95 wording if a player queries it.
- **Lend a Hand overlaps Core Support (p.20).** Kept because it is camp-only and costs the helper's task.
- **Tend a Wound** overrides Core's rule that Wounded heroes gain no Endurance on a Short Rest. Intentional.
- **Dunlending Survivor Virtues** not checked against the boons.
- **Untested:** the whole thing. Note which task each player actually chooses, and whether anyone ever takes Stand Guard or camps cold.

## Player handout

**Making Camp.** Ask to stop. Your rest works as normal. Camping is extra, and the gains are gone by the next camp.

- **Keep watch:** always rolled. Sharp watch means attackers start slow.
- **Cook** (fire): twice tomorrow, someone's armour holds better.
- **Sing** (fire): twice, the dark bites less. Sing a few lines and roll better.
- **Tell a tale:** ask one thing about the place ahead. A bad roll gets part of it. A very bad one has a lie in it, and you'll know.
- **Pick the spot:** helps the watch.
- **Tend a wound:** a hurt friend's rest counts. The wound stays.
- **Hold each other up:** the Fellowship regains a point, once per Adventuring Phase.
- **Stand guard:** no reward. Protects whoever you name.
- **Lend a hand:** skip your job, help another's.

Only three of you can take watch, cook, sing or tell a tale. Say what you're doing before anyone rolls. Nothing here stops a wound or changes the dice, and camp is never a safe refuge.
